# Reserve credit when a Single-Use Card is issued, not when it is charged

Issuing a Single-Use Card places a Hold for the full Invoice amount, so a Supplier's Charge on an approved Invoice can't be declined because the Buyer's other spending used up the credit in the meantime. The cost is that credit sits reserved for up to 30 days before any money moves; an unused card's Hold is released when the card expires.

**Amended (#54):** a card authorized near the end of its 30 days keeps its Hold for up to 7 days after its Authorization, until a Clearing or the 7-day rule.
